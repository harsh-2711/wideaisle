## Owner's note (the decision)

$150

## Risk

The top of the launch range (about $190) breaks the cap. Raising it from revenue is the owner's call at G3 or G4.

## What this means for agents

- Every memo carries a monthly cost estimate.
- If the cap is about to be crossed, pause non-critical lanes and send a memo instead of spending.
- The weekly brief reports spend and headroom against the cap.
