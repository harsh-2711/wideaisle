## Decision

Free personalised reports to stores on supported themes. The owner approves each batch. Stores recently named in lawsuits are excluded from cold outreach.

## Options

| Option | What | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| A | Personalised free reports to stores on supported themes; the owner approves each batch; include or exclude stores recently named in lawsuits | $0 |

## Agents' pick

Exclude sued stores from cold outreach

## Risk

Outreach can feel like spam or ambulance chasing. Reports must be genuinely useful, and every opt-out is honoured.

## What this means for agents

- Agents draft every message. Only the owner sends.
- Keep a suppression list for opt-outs and sued stores.
