## Decision

Free scan, Starter $29 a month, Pro $79 a month, Agency $199 a month for up to 10 stores. Pilots get a founding price, for example 50% off for 12 months.

| Option | What | Cost |

## Risk

Adafix sits at $29. We compete on the theme library, evidence pack and regression watch.

## What this means for agents

- Billing goes through the Shopify Billing API only (M6). Charging outside it is a common rejection reason.
- Infrastructure plus AI per store should stay under 10% of its plan price.
