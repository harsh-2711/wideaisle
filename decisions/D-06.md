## Decision

A: never claim compliant, certified, lawsuit-proof or 100% accessible. Describe fixed issues and evidence only.

## Options

| Option | What | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| A | Never say compliant, certified or lawsuit-proof; describe fixed issues and evidence only | $0 |
| B | Softer marketing wording | $0 |

## Agents' pick

A

## Risk

Weaker marketing hooks than overlay vendors. Much lower legal risk: the FTC fined the largest overlay vendor $1M over compliance claims.

## What this means for agents

- Applies to code comments, UI copy, listing copy, emails, reports and the landing page.
- Say how many detected issues were fixed, what we keep watching and what documentation the merchant gets.
- Say plainly that automated tools cannot detect every WCAG failure.
