## Research result (T-014, 2026-10-08)

AI-driven checks can replace most of the repeatable part of a per-release
check, but not all of it. They cannot judge real usability, they do not
cover JAWS or phone screen readers, and LLM output is not stable enough
to be the only check. Full brief: docs/research/d13-ai-accessibility-checks.md.

Expert time uses one basis: 2 to 3 hours per theme at $80 to $200 an
hour, so $800 to $3,000 for all five themes.

| Option | What | Cost (est.) |
|---|---|---|
| A | Owner's 30-minute check only | $0 |
| B | Paid expert every release | $100 to $300 a release (the plan's estimate), about one theme's check |
| C | Monthly expert retainer | Not priced |
| D | AI checks every release (scripted VoiceOver and NVDA runs, LLM review of the accessibility tree, monthly keyboard-only agent) plus the owner's check, plus a paid expert check of all five themes at G2, G3, then quarterly or per new theme | API about $2 to $4 a release plus $6 to $25 a month for the keyboard agent. CI minutes: $0 while the repo is public; about $2 to $4 a release at list prices if it turns private and runs past the free allowance. Expert checks $800 to $3,000 each, $160 to $600 per new theme |

Over the first 12 releases (weekly, M4 to M7):

| Option | Cost (est.) |
|---|---|
| B at the plan's estimate | $1,200 to $3,600, about one theme a release |
| B covering all five themes every release | $9,600 to $36,000 |
| D | About $1,650 to $6,150: API $40 to $125, two expert checks $1,600 to $6,000, CI minutes $0 while public (up to about $45 if private) |

D is not cheaper than B at the plan's estimate over the first 12 releases.
It covers more: every theme on every release by machine, plus two full
expert checks with JAWS and iPhone VoiceOver. After launch, D costs about
$850 to $3,150 a quarter against $1,200 to $3,600 for B at 12 releases a
quarter.

### Agents' proposal, pending the owner's pick (Q-30)

D. It checks every theme on every release by machine and keeps paid
experts for the moments that matter, with JAWS and phone screen readers
added there. It costs about the same as B, or more, in the first three
months.

### Risk

AI checks may miss a serious barrier in a covered flow. Before G2, run
the AI checks and an expert on the same golden store; if the AI misses a
serious barrier, fall back to B until the gap is closed.

### What this means for agents

If the owner picks D:

- Lane G builds Guidepup and Playwright screen-reader flows per golden
  store in M4 and gates CI on changes to spoken output.
- The LLM review reports warnings only. It never gates a release.

- Reports say "automated and AI-assisted checks" and list what was not covered (D-06).

If the owner picks B, a paid expert checks each release, and each check
still reaches the owner as a purchase memo first (D-04).
