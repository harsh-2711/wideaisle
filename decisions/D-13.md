## Decision

First test whether an AI check (for example, an agent driving a screen reader or keyboard-only walk-through) can replace the human check at low cost. If not, hire a freelancer per release.

## Options

| Option | What | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| A | None | $0 |
| B | A certified freelancer per release | About $100 to $300 per release (est.) |
| C | A monthly retainer | Higher (est.) |

## Owner's note (the decision)

Check if this can be automated with AI in a cost-effective way, else per release

## Risk

AI checks may miss the barriers automated tools already miss. The owner still does about 30 minutes of screen-reader checks per release.

## What this means for agents

- Plan a research task: AI-driven keyboard and screen-reader checks, what they catch, and cost per release.
- Bring the result back as a memo with a pick before G2.
