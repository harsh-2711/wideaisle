## Decision

Wide Aisle (WideAisle), tagline "Make room for every shopper".

## Options

| Option | What | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| A | Agents shortlist 10 names with domain and trademark checks; the name cannot contain "Shopify" | Domain about $10 to $20 a year (est.) |

## Agents' pick

Pick for now a short catchy name related to the problem or the solution. It should sound friendly.

## Risk

The name may clash with an existing trademark. Check before buying anything.

## What this means for agents

- Check wideaisle.com, wideaisle.app and getwideaisle.com, and run a trademark search. Report results in a memo.
- Do not buy a domain. The owner buys it (D-04).
- Never put "Shopify" in the name, listing title or domain.
